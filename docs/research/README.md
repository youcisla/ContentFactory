## Rules

1. A digest may be edited when new facts arrive; the underlying report may not (ADR-0005).
2. Every digest statement carries either a report reference or an explicit `[E]` estimate label.
3. Star counts, prices, and licence terms are point-in-time. The date is stated wherever they appear.
4. No digest may introduce a claim that no report supports.
